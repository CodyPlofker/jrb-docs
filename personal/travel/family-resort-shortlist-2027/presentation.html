Before picking a resort

Can we agree on this?

If the answer to these is yes, the shortlist gets much easier.

Yes #1

Kids need their own fun.

Not just a pool. A real kids club, waterpark, teen room, sports, crafts, or camp structure.

Yes #2

Adults need breaks.

A few hours at the pool, bar, spa, beach, or long lunch without being the entertainment committee.

Yes #3

Logistics should be boring.

Easy flights, easy meals, easy transfers, and no daily debate about what twelve people are doing.

Why the kids say yes

The kids need to see a trip that feels built for them.

The sell

The kids club is not a footnote. It is the reason this can feel like a real vacation for everyone.

  • Little kids get toys, crafts, splash areas, movies, and supervised play.
  • Older kids get games, sports, hangout spaces, and a little independence.
  • The group gets fewer “what are we doing now?” moments every day.
Why adults say yes

If the kids are happy, adults can actually exhale.

This is what we are buying.

Not just a nicer room. A setup where adults can have pool-bar, spa, beach, or long-lunch time while the kids are somewhere they want to be.

Finest puts it plainly: “Mom and Dad can sip cocktails under a beachside thatched-roof lounge while junior gives windsurfing a whirl.”

What we are pitching

An all-inclusive with a real kids club.

We are looking for the place where kids have a built-in day, parents can relax without planning every hour, and the whole group can just show up.

1

Kids club first

Supervised programming, splash/play spaces, and enough activity that kids actually want to go.

2

All-inclusive second

Meals, drinks, pool, beach, and low-friction logistics for a big family/friend group.

Two directions

Same goal, different vibe.

Direction A: warm all-inclusive beach

Mexico / Caribbean. Best if the dream is drop kids, pool, drinks, beach, dinner, repeat.

  • Most “vacation” feeling
  • Easiest sell to the group
  • Watch July heat + hurricane season flexibility

Direction B: cooler family-camp

Vermont / Québec / Poconos / Hudson Valley. Less tropical, more structure, often easier logistics.

  • Better summer weather
  • More activities, lake, horses, bikes
  • Less luxury-resort, more camp-with-cocktails
