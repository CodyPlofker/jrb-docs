Two trip lanes

Decide the type of vacation before arguing resort tabs.

Option A — Warm all-inclusive beach trip

Where: Cancun / Playa Mujeres / Riviera Maya, Turks & Caicos, Punta Cana.

  • Best if the group values “drop kids, sit by pool, no planning.”
  • Feels like a real adult vacation: restaurants, beach, spa, bars.
  • Watchout: June/July heat, storms, and sunscreen logistics.

Option B — Cooler family-camp trip

Where: Vermont, Hudson Valley, Poconos, Québec.

  • Better July weather for active kids: lake, bikes, horses, camp.
  • Often easier from Northeast / Midwest with less passport friction.
  • Less luxe-tropical; more summer-camp-with-cocktails.
