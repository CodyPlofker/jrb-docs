Two trip lanes

Use this to make the group decision easier before getting buried in resort tabs.

Option A — Warm all-inclusive beach trip

Where: Cancun / Playa Mujeres / Riviera Maya, Turks & Caicos, Punta Cana.

  • Feels like a real vacation for adults: pools, beach, drinks, restaurants, spa.
  • Best if the group values “drop kids, sit by pool, no planning.”
  • June/July will be hot; Mexico / Caribbean also means summer storm risk and more sunscreen logistics.

Option B — Domestic / cooler “family camp” trip

Where: Vermont, Hudson Valley, Poconos, Québec.

  • Often easier from Northeast / Midwest with less passport friction.
  • Better weather for active kids in July: lake, bikes, horses, camp, sports.
  • Less luxe-tropical, more “summer camp with cocktails after bedtime.”

Suggested next move

Send the group a simple vote with two questions:

  1. Warm beach all-inclusive vs cooler family-camp all-inclusive?
  2. If warm: rank Dreams Playa Mujeres, Finest Playa Mujeres, Beaches Turks & Caicos, Club Med Punta Cana.

Before deposits

  • Get departure cities and kid ages from every family.
  • Ask each resort: exact unsupervised drop-off minimum, potty-training rules, hours, capacity, and whether pre-registration is required.
  • Request 5-night room-block quotes for late June and mid-July.
  • Check cancellation / hurricane-season flexibility.
