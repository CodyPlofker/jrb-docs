Trip strategy

Pick the lane before the hotel.

Warm all-inclusive beach

Mexico / Caribbean. Best if the group wants to drop kids, sit by the pool, and avoid planning every meal.

  • Adult vacation feel: beach, pools, restaurants, spa, bars.
  • Most relevant to the original ask.
  • Watchout: June/July heat, storms, and transfer logistics.

Cooler family-camp

Northeast / Québec. Better weather, less passport friction, and more daytime activity structure.

  • Lake, bikes, horses, camp, sports.
  • Often easier for Northeast families.
  • Less luxury-resort, more summer-camp-with-cocktails.
