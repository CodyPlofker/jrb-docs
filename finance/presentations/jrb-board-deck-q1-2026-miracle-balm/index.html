What fans say glow, dewy finish, moisture, “my skin but better,” mature-skin friendliness.

What detractors say greasy/sticky/heavy feel, not enough color or coverage, shade mismatch, smell/texture objections.

The polarization is not about awareness; it is about expectation-setting. Some customers buy a makeup product and receive a skin-finish balm.

Implication for board discussion

Miracle Balm needs clearer segmentation, not just more promotion.

1. Keep minis, but define their job: trial / entry point / gifting, not replacement economics.

2. Reframe full-size Miracle Balm around the customers who love the finish instead of treating it as universally self-explanatory.

3. Fix expectation-setting: balm, sheen, no-coverage, texture, shade payoff. The review negatives are predictable and addressable.
