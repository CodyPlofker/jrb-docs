Description

What The Foundation is our take on a foundation, offering light-medium, buildable coverage. Its ultra-nourishing formula blends seamlessly into the skin, leaving you with an even, fresh complexion that looks like your skin on its best day. WTF works flawlessly with The Face Pencil for added coverage in areas with extra redness, blemishes or discoloration.

Non-comedogenic. Due to the unique formula, some shifting may occur in transit.

How to use

Use your index finger or the spatula to lift a pea-sized amount of WTF. Apply directly to your face, avoiding the eyes, using your fingers, the Skin Brush, or a dry sponge. Layer as needed. Follow up with The Face Pencil or The Neutralizer Pencil for added coverage on dark spots, redness or blemishes, and to conceal under-eye darkness.

Pro tip: Since WTF offers rich nourishment, try skipping your usual moisturizer before applying it.

Bobbi's tip: Some people may need two shades — one for summer, one for winter, or a mix of both for the times in between.

Key ingredients

Sodium Hyaluronate — the super moisturizer that helps retain moisture, improve elasticity and firmness of dry skin, minimizes the appearance of fine lines, and hydrates for a supple look.

Tocopheryl Acetate — an antioxidant that helps protect against environmental stressors while strengthening the skin’s moisture barrier.

Made with botanical oils (jojoba, sunflower, castor seed, geranium, ginger & olive) to soothe, calm and nourish.
