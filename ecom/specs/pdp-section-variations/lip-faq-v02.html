The questions we hear most

No accordion, no scrolling for answers. Here's the straight story on color, scent, and feel.

QIs there actually color, or is it basically clear?

There's real color — just a sheer wash of it. All 9 shades are casually sheer and universal. Even brighter-looking shades like Lilac sheer out to a flattering wash that never looks overdone. See the on-lip swatches above for each shade.

QWhat does it smell or taste like?

A subtle vanilla mint scent. The cooling, minty note comes from real peppermint and spearmint oils — light, fresh, never overpowering.

QWhat does it feel like — is it sticky?

Not sticky. It's a cushiony, moisturizing balm feel that melts in with shea butter, vitamin E, and peptides — comfortable and weightless, never tacky or heavy.

QWhat's the finish?

A soft shine finish. Not as glossy as a traditional lip gloss, but a subtle shine that leaves lips looking healthy and cared for.

QHow is this different from a lip gloss?

It's a more skincare-forward formula — powered by moisture-boosting peptides, vitamin E, and shea butter. It adds a hint of color while smoothing and softening lips as you wear it.

QHow do I pick a shade?

You almost can't go wrong — every shade is universal. Want barely-there? Start with On The Rocks or Champagne. Want a bit more? Try Rosé, Petal, or Sangria.
