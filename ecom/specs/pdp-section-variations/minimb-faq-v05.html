The thinking behind Miracle Balm

Miracle Balm is a light-reflecting balm for a fresh, healthy glow — a highlighter, blush, and bronzer in one. The mini is the same formula in a travel size, so here's how we think about the questions we hear most.

What's the trick to using it?

"Genius in a pot. Once you break the seal of the balm, you can wear it alone, or use it to bronze, highlight, or tint the skin by mixing and matching shades."

— Bobbi's Tip

Will it look greasy, or give any real color?

Neither. It's a sheer wash of color that melts in to leave skin feeling smooth and comfortable — never greasy. A little goes a long way, so start small and build to the glow you want.

— Jones Road, on Miracle Balm

How is the mini different from the full size?

It's the exact same formula — just a smaller travel- and trial-size jar. It's the easiest way to test a shade or keep one in your bag. If you love it, the full size is the better value per use.

— Jones Road, on Mini Miracle Balm

Is it good for dry skin?

Yes. It's made with skin-loving ingredients and melts in to leave skin feeling smooth, moisturized, and comfortable — a glow, not a powdery finish.

— Jones Road, on Miracle Balm

We took Bobbi's expertise and distilled it into our Miracle Balm Quiz, making it simple to find your perfect shade.
