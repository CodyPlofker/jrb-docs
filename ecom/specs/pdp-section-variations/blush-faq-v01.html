Blushing Stick

Honest answers before you buy

How do I apply it, and how much do I use?
Twist the stick up just a little, then swipe directly onto lips and cheeks. Blend on cheeks with your fingers or a brush, building up as you like. The color goes on light and builds fast, so a little goes a long way — start with one pass and add more for a deeper flush.
Is it worth it for the size?
It's a buttery cream blush and lip tint in one, so it does the work of two products from a single stick. Because the formula is so pigmented, a single swipe is enough — most people reach for it daily, and a little keeps it lasting. (4.5g / 0.16 oz.)
Which shade should I pick — will the color suit me?
Every shade goes on as a buildable wash of color, so it adjusts to how much you apply rather than overpowering your skin. Cooler tones (Bare, Flush, Pinch) read rosy; warmer tones (Miami, Cheeky) read peachy-to-berry; Chic and Pretty are everyday pinks. Start sheer and build to the flush you want.
Can I wear it on both lips and cheeks?
Yes — it's designed as a lip and cheek tint in one. Swipe onto lips for a tint and dab onto cheeks for a matching flush, so your whole look stays effortless and pulled together.
Will it feel dry or heavy on my skin?
No. It's a buttery formula made to melt into skin and help support its moisture barrier for a happy, healthy flush — comfortable enough to wear all day on both lips and cheeks.
How long does it take to do my look?
Seconds, not minutes. Swipe, blend with a fingertip, and you're done — whether you keep it subtle or build it up.
