The Face Pencil

The three things people ask first

The worry

"Will it drag, cake, or settle into under-eye lines?"

Straight answer

Only if you drag it — so don't. The formula is creamy and dense; warm it and pat it in with a fingertip instead of pulling the pencil across the skin. Thin, buildable layers stay smooth and natural, and a clear base means no chalky undertones.

The worry

"Which shade do I pick — how do I match?"

Straight answer

Match it to your skin. Bobbi's tip: look for the shade that disappears on your cheek and the shade that disappears on your forehead — they're not always the same. Having both on hand keeps you covered for every area.

The worry

"How much coverage will I actually get?"

Straight answer

Light-to-medium and buildable. It's made to spot-conceal redness, blemishes, and uneven tone while staying virtually undetectable. Use it only where you need it and add a thin layer for more — a little goes a long way.
