Between two shades? Here's how to pick

  • Go cooler (Petal, Pink Lilac, Dahlia) for a fresh, your-lips-but-better pink.
  • Go warmer (Champagne, Me) when you want a soft nude-gold that disappears into your tone.
  • Want it to actually read? Sangria and Lilac give the most payoff — still sheer, never overdone.
  • Truly can't decide? On The Rocks is clear — pure shine, zero color commitment.

Remember: even the boldest shades sheer out on contact and leave a wash of color that never looks heavy. You can't go wrong.
