The thinking behind WTF

What The Foundation is our take on a foundation — built to look like your skin on its best day, not a mask on top of it. Here's how Bobbi thinks about the questions we hear most.

Why make a foundation that's only light-medium?

Because the goal was never to hide your skin. WTF offers light-medium, buildable coverage, with an ultra-nourishing formula that blends seamlessly into the skin — leaving you with an even, fresh complexion that looks like your skin on its best day.

— Jones Road, on What The Foundation

What if I want more coverage in some spots?

Then build it. Start with a small amount to even out your complexion, then go in with a bit more — or reach for The Face Pencil for added coverage in areas with extra redness, blemishes, or discoloration.

— Jones Road, on What The Foundation

How is this different from a traditional foundation?

Traditional foundations tend to feel heavy, cakey, or uncomfortable. Instead, WTF gives your skin a natural, fresh, and glowing look. It's silky, smooth, and won't dry down or accentuate lines. It's like your skin on its best day, every day.

— Jones Road, on What The Foundation

How do I land on the right shade?

We took Bobbi's expertise and distilled it into our complexion shade-matching quiz, making it simple to find your perfect shade.

— Jones Road, on What The Foundation
