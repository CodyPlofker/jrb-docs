Lip Recharge

What it is, and what it isn't

A skincare-forward lip treatment with a sheer wash of color — here's how to set your expectations.

What it is

  • A sheer wash of color in 9 universal, casually sheer shades that flatter every lip tone
  • A cushiony, moisturizing feel that melts in with shea butter, vitamin E, and moisture-boosting peptides
  • A soft shine finish that looks healthy and cared for
  • A subtle vanilla mint scent with a cooling metal applicator
  • A treatment you can throw on without a mirror — nourishment, tint, and shine in one

What it isn't

  • Not opaque or full-coverage — even brighter shades sheer out to a flattering wash, never overdone
  • Not sticky or tacky — it won't feel heavy on your lips
  • Not a high-shine lip gloss — the shine is soft and subtle, not glassy
  • Not a purely cosmetic gloss — it's a more skincare-forward formula that works while you wear it
