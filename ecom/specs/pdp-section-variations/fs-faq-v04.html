Your Skin Foundation Stick

What it is, what it isn't

What it is

  • Medium-to-full, buildable. The fullest coverage of our complexion line — add more without ever looking overdone.
  • A natural skin finish. Smooth and natural — you look like you, having a great skin day.
  • Lightweight and comfortable. As light as a stick can get — 100% of users agreed it never felt heavy or cakey.
  • Built for on-the-go. A stick you swipe on and blend with your fingers, anywhere.
  • Good for your skin. Dermatologist-tested, non-comedogenic, with ceramides, squalane and sodium hyaluronate.

What it isn't

  • Not heavy, cakey, or mask-like — even at full coverage.
  • Not a sheer tint. If you want lighter, that's What The Foundation (light-to-medium) or Just Enough (light).
  • Not a one-size shade. There are 30 to choose from, lightest to deepest.
  • Not drying — it's a creamy formula that puts your skin first.

Between Your Skin and What The Foundation? Reach for Your Skin on the days you want more coverage; reach for What The Foundation when you want something lighter. Many people keep both.
