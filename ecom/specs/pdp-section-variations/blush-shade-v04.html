Between two shades? Here's how to pick

Go with the one that pulls toward your natural flush — pinch your cheek and match that tone. Cooler skin tends to love the pinks and berries; warm skin glows in coral.

Still split? Choose the deeper of the two. Blushing Stick starts light and builds, so you can always apply a deeper shade with a lighter hand — but you can't add depth to a shade that's too soft.

A little goes a long way — one stick does cheeks and lips.
