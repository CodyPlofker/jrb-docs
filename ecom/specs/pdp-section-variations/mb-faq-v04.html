Miracle Balm

What to expect from your balm

What it is

  • A light-reflecting balm for a sheer wash of color and a fresh, healthy glow
  • A highlighter, blush, bronzer — and so much more, depending on the shade
  • A formula that melts in to leave skin feeling smooth, moisturized, and comfortable
  • Tap on cheeks, lips, or wear as an all-over tint, alone or layered over foundation

What it isn't

  • Not greasy or heavy — it melts into skin instead of sitting on top
  • Not a high-pigment, full-color makeup — it's a sheer, natural tint
  • Not one-and-done by the handful — a little goes a long way
  • Not for the eyes or eyelids

New to it? Start with the smallest amount, warm it between your fingers, and tap it on — you can always add more for a touch more glow.
