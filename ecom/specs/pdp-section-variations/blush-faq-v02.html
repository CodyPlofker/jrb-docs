Blushing Stick

Everything you want to know

How do I apply it, and how much do I use?

Twist up just a little, swipe onto lips and cheeks, then blend with a finger or brush. It goes on light and builds fast, so a little goes a long way — start with one pass and add more.

Is it worth it for the size?

It's a cream blush and lip tint in one, doing the work of two products from a single stick. The formula is pigmented, so one swipe is enough and a little keeps it lasting. (4.5g / 0.16 oz.)

Which shade should I pick?

Every shade is a buildable wash of color, so it adjusts to how much you apply. Cool tones read rosy, warm tones read peachy-to-berry, and Chic and Pretty are everyday pinks. Start sheer and build.

Can I really use it on lips and cheeks?

Yes — it's a lip and cheek tint in one. Swipe onto lips for a tint and dab onto cheeks for a matching flush, so your whole look stays effortless.

Will it feel dry or heavy?

No. It's a buttery formula made to melt into skin and help support its moisture barrier — comfortable enough to wear all day on both lips and cheeks.

How long does my look take?

Seconds, not minutes. Swipe, blend with a fingertip, and you're done — whether you keep it subtle or build it up.
