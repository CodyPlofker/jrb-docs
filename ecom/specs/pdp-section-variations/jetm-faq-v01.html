Just Enough Tinted Moisturizer

Honest answers before you buy

What coverage and finish does Just Enough give?
Just Enough is a lightweight tinted moisturizer with a soft touch of coverage and a soft, natural finish. It smooths and evens skin tone, hides redness, looks natural, and feels like nothing on your skin. It's sheerer than What The Foundation — think your skin, just more even.
Does Just Enough have SPF?
No — Just Enough does not contain SPF. It's a tinted moisturizer that evens tone and adds a soft touch of coverage. Layer your own sunscreen underneath as the last step of your skincare, then apply Just Enough on top.
Will the shade match me — and which is lightest for fair skin?
There are 16 flexible shades, and the lightest is Alabaster — the best starting point for fair skin. Take our complexion shade matching quiz, designed by Bobbi to help you choose your perfect shade. If you're between two, the lighter one warms slightly as it settles into skin.
How does this differ from What The Foundation?
Compared to What The Foundation, Just Enough offers lighter weight moisture, lighter coverage, and a lighter finish yet is still effective at covering redness and evening out the complexion on all skin types. We recommend having both in your makeup bag and switching out based on your desired look, the season, or whatever your skin is craving that day.
What step should I apply it in my routine?
Just Enough Tinted Moisturizer should be applied after you've finished your skincare, as the very first step in your makeup routine. Follow up with The Face Pencil for added coverage on stubborn dark spots or extra redness.
Will this clog my pores?
No, Just Enough Tinted Moisturizer is non-comedogenic and features clean, skin-friendly ingredients.
