So what kind of coverage and finish is it?

B

It's a lightweight tinted moisturizer with just a soft touch of coverage and a soft, natural finish — sheerer than What The Foundation. It smooths and evens skin tone, hides redness, and feels like nothing on. That's the whole point: even skin that still reads as your skin.

Does it have SPF?

B

No — Just Enough doesn't contain SPF. I kept it to tint and moisture. Put your sunscreen on as the last step of your skincare, then layer Just Enough right on top.

I have fair skin — where do I start?

B

Start with Alabaster, the lightest of the 16 shades. Take my complexion shade matching quiz to confirm — and if you're ever between two, the lighter one warms slightly as it settles into skin.

When should I reach for this over What The Foundation?

B

Just Enough is lighter in weight, coverage, and finish. I tell people to keep both and switch based on the look you want, the season, or whatever your skin is craving that day.
