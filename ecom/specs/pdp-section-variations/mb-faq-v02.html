Miracle Balm

Everything you want to know

Will it look greasy, or give any real color?

Neither. It's a sheer wash of color that melts in to leave skin smooth and comfortable — never greasy. A little goes a long way, so start small and add more for a touch more glow.

How do I use it, and how much?

Break the top surface with your index finger, warm it to soften, then tap onto cheeks, lips, or anywhere you want to glow. A little goes a long way — build up to the look you want.

What products does Miracle Balm replace?

Depending on the shade, Miracle Balm can replace your blush, bronzer, highlighter, color corrector, or even your moisturizer.

Can I wear it with other makeup products?

Absolutely — you can layer Miracle Balm over or under any product in your routine. Or wear it alone for a natural, no-makeup makeup glow.

Will this clog my pores?

No, Miracle Balm is non-comedogenic and features clean, skin-friendly ingredients.
