Mini Miracle Balm

Everything you want to know, answered

Will it look greasy, or give real color?

Neither. It's a sheer wash of color that melts in to leave skin smooth and comfortable — never greasy. Start small and build to the glow you want.

How is the mini different from the full size?

It's the same exact formula, just a smaller travel- and trial-size jar (0.10 oz). Perfect for testing a shade or your bag; the full size (0.55 oz) is the better value per use.

How do I use it?

Break the surface with your finger, warm a tiny amount to soften it, then tap onto cheeks or lips with your fingertips. A little goes a long way.

Which shade should I pick?

Pinks and roses read as a fresh flush; golden and bronze tones warm the skin. Not sure? Our Miracle Balm Quiz, built from Bobbi's expertise, makes the match simple.

What does it replace?

Depending on the shade, it's a highlighter, blush, and bronzer in one — and can stand in for a color corrector too.

Is it good for dry skin?

Yes. Made with skin-loving ingredients, it melts in to leave skin feeling smooth, moisturized, and comfortable. It's also non-comedogenic.

Will it clog my pores?

No — Miracle Balm is non-comedogenic and made with clean, skin-friendly ingredients.

Can I wear it with other makeup?

Absolutely. Layer it over or under anything in your routine, or wear it alone for a no-makeup glow.
