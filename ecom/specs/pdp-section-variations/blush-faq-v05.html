The thinking behind Blushing Stick

Blushing Stick is a buttery lip and cheek tint that starts light, blends seamlessly, and builds naturally — your whole flush from one stick. Here's how we think about the questions we hear most.

Why a blush in the first place?

"When all else fails, a little blush always helps — it's an instant mood booster."

— Bobbi's Tip

How do I apply it, and how much do I use?

Twist up just a little, then swipe directly onto lips and cheeks and blend with your fingers or a brush, building up as you like. It goes on light and builds fast, so a little goes a long way.

— Jones Road, on Blushing Stick

Is it really worth it for the size?

It's a cream blush and lip tint in one, so it does the work of two products from a single stick. The formula is pigmented, so one swipe is enough and a little keeps it lasting. (4.5g / 0.16 oz.)

— Jones Road, on Blushing Stick

Which shade will suit me?

Every shade goes on as a buildable wash of color, so it adjusts to how much you apply rather than overpowering your skin. Start sheer and build to the flush you want.

— Jones Road, on Blushing Stick
