Lip Recharge

Honest answers before you buy

Is there actually color, or is it basically clear?
There's real color — just a sheer wash of it. Lip Recharge comes in 9 universal, casually sheer shades anyone can wear. Even shades that look brighter in the tube, like Lilac, sheer out once applied and leave a wash of color that never looks overdone. Check the on-lip swatches above to see how each shade reads on real lips.
What does it smell or taste like?
Lip Recharge features a subtle vanilla mint scent. The mint comes from real peppermint and spearmint oils, which give it a light, cooling feel as you apply.
What does it feel like — is it sticky?
No. It's a cushiony, moisturizing balm feel — soft and comfortable, never tacky or heavy. The cooling metal applicator glides on, and the formula melts in with shea butter, vitamin E, and moisture-boosting peptides to smooth and soften lips while you wear it.
What's the finish — is it glossy?
Lip Recharge provides a soft shine finish. It's not as glossy as a traditional lip gloss, but leaves lips with a subtle amount of shine that looks healthy and cared for.
How is this different from a traditional lip gloss?
Lip Recharge features a more skincare-forward formula than a cosmetic lip product does. It's powered by high-performance ingredients like moisture-boosting peptides, vitamin E, and shea butter. While it adds a flattering hint of color, it's also working to smooth and soften lips as you wear it.
How do I know which shade is right for me?
You almost can't go wrong — all 9 shades are universal and casually sheer. If you want a barely-there tint, start with On The Rocks or Champagne. For a touch more color, try Rosé, Petal, or Sangria. Whatever you choose sheers out to a flattering, natural wash.
