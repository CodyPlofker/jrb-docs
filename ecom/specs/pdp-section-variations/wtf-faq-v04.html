What The Foundation

What it is. What it isn't.

So you know exactly what's arriving before you open the box.

What it is

  • Light-medium, buildable coverage you control
  • An ultra-nourishing formula that looks like skin, not makeup
  • At its best on normal-to-dry skin
  • A fresh, glowing finish that won't accentuate lines
  • Non-comedogenic, clean, skin-friendly ingredients

What it isn't

  • A full-coverage, mask-it-all foundation
  • Heavy, cakey, or drying on the skin
  • A long-wear matte made for very oily skin
  • Something that settles into fine lines
  • A shade that oxidizes dark as it sets
Want more coverage? Build a second layer where you need it, or pair WTF with The Face Pencil for redness, blemishes, and discoloration.
