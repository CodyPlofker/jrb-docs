What The Foundation

The three things people get wrong

You might think

"It'll give me full, flawless coverage."

WTF is light-medium and buildable — designed to look like skin, not makeup. Start with a small scoop to even things out, then build a little more, or spot-conceal with The Face Pencil, exactly where you want it. If you want heavy, full-mask coverage, this isn't that — and that's the point.

You might think

"I'll never get the right shade."

We took Bobbi's expertise and distilled it into our complexion shade-matching quiz, so finding your match is simple. Between two shades? Go lighter — the formula warms slightly as it settles into skin, so it tends to meet you in the middle rather than oxidizing dark.

You might think

"It won't hold up on my oily skin."

Honestly — WTF is an ultra-nourishing, moisturizing formula, so it's at its best on normal-to-dry skin. If your skin runs oily, set it with a light powder, or reach for a sheerer tinted option on the days your skin needs less moisture. We'd rather tell you straight than have it not work for you.
