The Face Pencil

Honest answers before you buy

Will it drag, cake, or settle into under-eye lines?
It shouldn't — the formula is creamy and dense, so the trick is to warm it and pat, not drag. Tap a little on with your finger and gently press it into the skin rather than pulling the pencil across delicate areas. Build coverage in thin layers only where you need it, and the finish stays smooth and natural — never chalky, thanks to a clear base with no chalky undertones. A light dusting of Tinted Face Powder on top keeps it in place for longer wear.
How do I find my shade?
Match it to your skin, not your wish list. Bobbi's tip: look for the shade that disappears on your cheek and the shade that disappears on your forehead — they're not always the same. Having both on hand keeps you covered no matter what area of the face you're concealing. A lighter shade can also be used to brighten under the eyes.
How much coverage does it give?
It's a light-to-medium, buildable concealer designed to spot-conceal redness, blemishes, and uneven tone while staying virtually undetectable. Use it where you actually need it — around the nose and mouth — and add another thin layer for more coverage. It's a makeup artist's dream, but simple enough for a newbie to master.
How do I apply it?
After your skincare and moisturizer, start around the nose to conceal any redness. Next, apply around the mouth, spot-correcting as needed and tapping to blend the product into your skin. Build up coverage with multiple thin layers, then finish with a light dusting of Tinted Face Powder for longer-lasting wear.
Can I use it under my eyes?
Yes. A lighter shade can be used to lighten and brighten the under-eye area. Because the formula is creamy, warm it first and pat it in gently with a fingertip rather than dragging — that keeps it from settling into fine lines.
