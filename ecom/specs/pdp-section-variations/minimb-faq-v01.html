Mini Miracle Balm

Honest answers before you buy

Will it look greasy, or give any real color?
Neither. Mini Miracle Balm gives a sheer wash of color and melts in to leave skin feeling smooth and comfortable — never greasy. A little goes a long way, so start with the smallest amount, warm it between your fingers, and tap it on. You can always add more for a touch more glow.
How is the mini different from the full size?
It's the exact same formula — just a smaller, travel- and trial-size jar (0.10 oz vs 0.55 oz in the full size). It's the easiest way to test a shade or toss one in your bag, and it fits TSA carry-on rules. If you love it, the full size is the better value per use.
How do I use it, and how much do I apply?
Break the top surface of the balm with your finger first, then warm a tiny amount to soften the formula. Tap it on with your fingertips onto cheeks, lips, or anywhere you want to tint or glow. A little goes a long way — start small and build up to the look you want.
Which shade should I pick?
Shades range from soft pinks to warm bronzes. As a guide: pinks and roses (like Dusty Rose or Flushed) read as a fresh flush; golden and bronze tones (like Golden Hour or Bronze) warm the skin like a bronzer. What does it replace in my routine?
Depending on the shade, Miracle Balm can stand in for your blush, bronzer, highlighter, or color corrector — a highlighter, blush, and bronzer in one little pot.
Is it good for dry skin?
Yes. It's made with skin-loving ingredients and melts in to leave skin feeling smooth, moisturized, and comfortable — a fresh, healthy glow rather than a powdery finish. It's also non-comedogenic.
