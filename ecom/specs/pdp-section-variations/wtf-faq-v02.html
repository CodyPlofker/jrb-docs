How much coverage will I actually get?

WTF has light-medium, buildable coverage — it looks like skin, not makeup. Scoop a small amount with The Spatula to even out your complexion, then build a bit more, or use The Face Pencil, where you want fuller coverage.

How do I find my shade?

We took Bobbi's expertise and distilled it into our complexion shade-matching quiz, making it simple to find your perfect shade. Between two shades? The lighter one warms slightly as it settles into skin.

Will this work on oily skin?

WTF is an ultra-nourishing, moisturizing formula, so it's at its best on normal-to-dry skin. If your skin runs oily, set it with a light powder or reach for a sheerer option on the days your skin needs less moisture.

How is it different from traditional foundation?

Traditional foundations tend to feel heavy, cakey, or uncomfortable. WTF's ultra-nourishing formula gives skin a natural, fresh, glowing look. It's silky, smooth, and won't dry down or accentuate lines — like your skin on its best day, every day.

How is it different from Just Enough?

Compared to Just Enough, WTF offers richer moisture, slightly more coverage, and a naturally glowing finish — both even out the complexion on all skin types. We recommend having both and switching based on the look, season, or whatever your skin is craving.

Will this clog my pores?

No, WTF is non-comedogenic and features clean, skin-friendly ingredients.
