Blushing Stick

What to expect from your stick

What it is

  • A buttery cream blush and lip tint in one — a flush for cheeks and a tint for lips from a single stick
  • A color that starts light, blends seamlessly, and builds naturally to the look you want
  • So pigmented that a little goes a long way — one swipe is usually plenty
  • A buttery formula that melts into skin and helps support its moisture barrier for a happy, healthy flush

What it isn't

  • Not a heavy, full-coverage blush — it's a sheer, buildable wash of color
  • Not a one-pass-and-done by the swipe — start small and build so it lasts
  • Not single-use — the same stick does both lips and cheeks
  • Not a slow routine — your look takes seconds, not minutes

New to it? Twist up just a little, swipe onto lips and cheeks, and blend with a fingertip — you can always add more for a deeper flush. (4.5g / 0.16 oz.)
