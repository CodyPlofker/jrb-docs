Will it drag, cake, or settle into my under-eye lines?

B

Only if you drag it — so don't. It's a creamy, dense formula, so warm it up and pat it into the skin with your fingertip instead of pulling the pencil across the delicate areas. Build coverage in thin layers, and finish with a light dusting of Tinted Face Powder for longer wear.

How do I pick the right shade?

B

Look for the shade that disappears on your cheek and the shade that disappears on your forehead — they are not always the same. Having both on hand will keep you covered no matter what areas of the face you are concealing.

Xx Bobbi

How much coverage should I expect?

B

It's light-to-medium and buildable — made to spot-conceal redness, blemishes, and uneven tone while staying virtually undetectable. Start around the nose, then the mouth, and only build up where you actually need it. A little goes a long way.
