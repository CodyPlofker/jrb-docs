Blushing Stick

The honest answers to your top 3 questions

The question

"How do I apply it, and how much do I use?"

Twist up just a little and swipe onto lips and cheeks, then blend with a finger or brush. It goes on light and builds fast, so a little goes a long way — start with one pass and add more for a deeper flush.

The question

"Is it worth it for the size?"

It's a buttery cream blush and lip tint in one, so it does the work of two products from a single stick. The formula is pigmented, so one swipe is enough and a little keeps it lasting. (4.5g / 0.16 oz.)

The question

"Which shade do I pick — will it suit me?"

Every shade is a buildable wash of color that adjusts to how much you apply. Cool tones read rosy, warm tones read peachy-to-berry, Chic and Pretty are everyday pinks. Start sheer and build to the flush you want.
