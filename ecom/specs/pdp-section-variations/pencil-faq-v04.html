The Face Pencil

What it is, and what it isn't

A creamy, dense concealer that conceals redness, blemishes, and uneven tone while staying virtually undetectable. Set expectations here, and you'll love it.

What it is

  • Light-to-medium, buildable spot coverage — use it only where you need it
  • Creamy and blendable, with a clear base so there are no chalky undertones
  • A way to brighten under the eyes with a lighter shade
  • A makeup artist's dream — but simple enough for a newbie to master

What it isn't

  • Not a full-face foundation — it's a targeted concealer
  • Not a heavy, full-coverage cake — a little goes a long way
  • Not something to drag across the skin — warm it and pat it in
  • Not one-shade-fits-all — match it to your skin, cheek and forehead

How to wear it so it never settles in lines

01

After moisturizer, start around the nose to conceal any redness.

02

Move around the mouth, spot-correcting and tapping to blend — never dragging.

03

Build with thin layers, then a light dusting of Tinted Face Powder for longer wear.
