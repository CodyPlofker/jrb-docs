The Face Pencil · FAQ

Everything you'd ask before your first one

Will it drag, cake, or settle into under-eye lines?

Not if you warm it and pat — don't drag the pencil. The formula is creamy and dense, so press it in gently with a fingertip instead of pulling across delicate skin. Thin, buildable layers stay smooth and natural, and a clear base means no chalky undertones.

Which shade should I get?

Match it to your skin. Bobbi's tip: find the shade that disappears on your cheek and the one that disappears on your forehead — they aren't always the same. Having both on hand keeps you covered for every area.

How much coverage does it give?

Light-to-medium and buildable. It's a creamy concealer that conceals redness, blemishes, and uneven tone while staying virtually undetectable. Spot-conceal where you need it and add a thin layer for more.

Can I use it under my eyes?

Yes — a lighter shade can be used to brighten the under-eye area. Warm it first and pat it in with a fingertip so it never settles into fine lines.

How do I apply it?

After moisturizer, start around the nose for redness, then around the mouth, spot-correcting and tapping to blend. Build with multiple thin layers and finish with a light dusting of Tinted Face Powder for longer wear.

Is it hard to use?

No. It's a makeup artist's dream, but simple enough for a newbie to master — the pencil format makes it easy to place exactly where you want it, no brush required.
