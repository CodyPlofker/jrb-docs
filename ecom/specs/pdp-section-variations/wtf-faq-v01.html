What The Foundation

Honest answers before you buy

How much coverage will I actually get?
WTF has light-medium, buildable coverage — it looks like skin, not makeup. Start by scooping a small amount with The Spatula to subtly even out your complexion, then apply a bit more, or go in with The Face Pencil, to achieve fuller coverage. It gives you the versatility to use it as your base in any kind of look.
How do I find the shade that's best for me?
We took Bobbi's expertise and distilled it into our complexion shade-matching quiz, making it simple to find your perfect shade. If you're between two shades, the lighter one warms slightly as it settles into skin.
Will this work on oily skin?
WTF is an ultra-nourishing, moisturizing formula, so it's at its best on normal-to-dry skin. If your skin runs oily, you may prefer to set it with a light powder, or reach for a sheer tinted option on the days your skin needs less moisture.
How does WTF differ from traditional foundation?
Traditional foundations tend to feel heavy, cakey, or uncomfortable. Instead, WTF offers an ultra-nourishing formula that gives your skin a natural, fresh, and glowing look. It's silky, smooth, and won't dry down or accentuate lines. It's like your skin on its best day, every day.
How does WTF differ from Just Enough Tinted Moisturizer?
Compared to Just Enough, What The Foundation offers richer moisture, slightly more coverage, and a naturally glowing finish — both are effective at covering redness and evening out the complexion on all skin types. We recommend having both in your makeup bag and switching out based on your desired look, the season, or whatever your skin is craving that day.
Will this clog my pores?
No, WTF is non-comedogenic and features clean, skin-friendly ingredients.
