TL;DR for the Wide Awake Strategic Doc

Pencil drag isn't a vibe — it's a quantified, repeatable, rating-killing friction across the JRB pencil portfolio. Wide Awake's creamy-pot format is the answer customers are already writing in their own words.

  • 1,545 reviews across Face Pencil + Neutralizer Pencil mention "dry." 449 use "drag" or "tug" verbatim. 431 say the pencil "pulls" on skin or eyelid.
  • "Pulls on skin / eyelid" reviews average 2.86★ on Face Pencil and 2.67★ on Neutralizer — vs. 4.24/4.28★ baselines. That's a ~1.4–1.6 star penalty per affected review.
  • ~10% of every 1–3★ review on Face Pencil and Neutralizer Pencil cites this exact friction. The drag complaint is the single largest rescuable bucket of negative reviews.
  • Lead Wide Awake's hero with comfort/glide, not just brightening. The audience is already telling us in their own words what they want fixed.
  • "Wide Awake vs. Neutralizer" comparison module on PDP isn't optional — it's the exact module customers ask for in 1–3★ reviews. Lead with format + comfort, not just the brightening complex.

Section 3 — Strategic implications for Wide Awake

Five places this data should show up in the strategy doc.

Hero claim hypothesis

"The brightening concealer that doesn't drag." Coverage that brightens, glides, and never tugs on under-eye skin — because 2.86 stars is what reviewers give a concealer that pulls.

01
Lead with comfort, then brightening
The brightening complex (marshmallow root + rice bran + licorice root) is the rational hook. But the data says comfort is the emotional hook — "too dry," "pulls on my skin," "tugs at my under eye" appear ~1,500+ times in the corpus. That's the language hero copy should answer first.
Hero claim · Ad copy
02
Audience is already self-identifying
10.1% of Face Pencil ≤3★ and 10.8% of Neutralizer ≤3★ reviewers describe their friction in the same vocabulary. That's a sized, addressable audience — ~700 verbatim-matched JRB customers who are pre-qualified Wide Awake buyers and reachable via post-purchase email today.
Launch audience · CRM
03
"Wide Awake vs. Neutralizer" PDP module
The Junip Deep-Dive flagged "How does Face Pencil differ from Neutralizer?" as the #1 unanswered education question. Wide Awake is the third entry in that confusion — it must lead its PDP with a side-by-side comparison: format, texture, when to use, who it's for. Pot vs. pencil, creamy vs. dry, brightening vs. neutralizing.
PDP · Education
04
Don't cannibalize — reposition
5,526 Neutralizer customers exist. The portfolio risk is splitting the under-eye line three ways. Use the drag data to pre-segment: Face Pencil = targeted concealer for blemishes/redness; Neutralizer = travel pencil for dark circles; Wide Awake = the everyday creamy concealer for women whose skin is too dry / mature / delicate for a pencil. Each product solves a different friction.
Portfolio · Positioning
05
UGC + ad brief direction
The "before / after" creative writes itself: finger swiping a pencil that drags on the eyelid → finger swiping Wide Awake that glides. Or: real reviewer reading a 1★ "tugs at my eye skin" review to camera, then trying Wide Awake. The verbatim quote bank in Section 2 is your shot list.
Creative · UGC
06
Future formulation read-across
Drag/dryness is also the #1 negative on Best Pencil and Brow Pencil per the Junip Expanded pull. That's a base-formula tell, not three independent product complaints. Worth a side-by-side hardness/glide test of the entire pencil line — Wide Awake's launch is the moment to either reformulate or reposition the pencils as targeted, not under-eye-default.

Method

How the counts were generated.

Source file: review_export_7180.csv from Junip — 362,745 lines, all JRB Yotpo + Junip reviews. Filtered to product_title = "The Face Pencil" (24,961 rows) and product_title = "The Neutralizer Pencil" (5,526 rows). Each phrase was matched against title + body using case-insensitive regex. "Pulls on skin/eyelid" requires the verb to appear within ~40 characters of skin/eye/lid/under to avoid false positives ("pulled it out of my bag"). Average ratings are mean of the rating column for matching reviews. The "Rating drop" column is the avg rating gap between the matched reviews and the product baseline (Face Pencil 4.24, Neutralizer 4.28). Verbatim quotes in Section 2 are pulled at random from 1–2★ reviews ≤260 chars matching the friction regex — unedited except for line-break removal.
