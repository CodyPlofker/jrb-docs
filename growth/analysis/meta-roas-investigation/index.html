What happened to ROAS

In-platform ROAS held 1.4-1.7× through Q4 2025, dipped to 1.27 in Feb, then dropped below 1.0 in March 2026 and stayed there. Three things broke at roughly the same time: (1) Meta P5 best-practices collapsed (Composite Optimal 99% → 51%, Advantage+ Creative 94% → 41%), (2) Meta switched conversion reporting on Mar 17, and (3) AEB campaigns were transitioned to Incremental Attribution (IA / broad reach). Reach is now 50%+ cheaper but link-click → purchase CVR has roughly halved.

Hypothesis tracker

From yesterday's Growth standup + Tommy's Slack message today (May 19). Ranked by my read of the data above.

  • Quiz pixel broken since May 12

    Quiz completion event stopped firing in Meta + GA4 on May 12. Six days of optimization-blind delivery on Quiz Complete ad sets. Confirmed by Growth team — action was to pause those campaigns.

    Maps to the late-May CPQ spike on the chart and contributes to May ROAS, but does not explain Mar-Apr decline.
  • P5 best-practice adoption collapsed (Feb 2026)

    Composite Optimal % went 99% (Dec '25) → 51% (May '26). Advantage+ Creative went 94% → 41%. Value/ROAS bid optimization went 63% → 19%. Spend in Learning Phase doubled to 16% (peaked 26% in Feb). When Meta loses signal hygiene, delivery quality drops first.

    Source: Tommy Washburn chart shared in #meta_jrb today. P5 collapse leads ROAS decline by ~30 days.
  • CAPI signal degraded May 2026

    CAPI Qualified % was 100% all year, dropped to 90% in May. Tommy flagged this in the same Slack message. Reduced server-side signal = Meta optimizing blind.

    Compounds with the broken Quiz pixel — broader Pixel/CAPI audit recommended.
  • AEB → IA transition pulled cheap impressions, not buyers

    Tommy recommended AEB → IA migration April 29. Late-April / May CPM crashed from $25 → $12 and impressions surged 50%+, but purchase CVR fell from 1.62% → 1.00%. Classic IA failure mode: broader audience, cheaper inventory, lower intent.

    Standup noted "downward trend may have started before IA was implemented" — IA accelerated, not caused, the decline.
  • Meta reporting change (March 17, 2026)

    Meta changed conversion reporting from "purchases counted on link clicks" to "purchases after link clicks." ROAS dipped below 1.0 within days of that change. Open question: how much is reporting artifact vs. real degradation? Likely both — comparing in-platform ROAS pre/post 3/17 conflates the two.

    To untangle: compare Northbeam-attributed ROAS over the same window — that doesn't move with Meta reporting changes. Standup said Northbeam ROAS hit ~4 (lowest ever), so real degradation is happening too.
  • Foundation Stick shade paralysis at conversion

    Standup: top-of-funnel engagement on Foundation Stick is healthy; drop-off is at conversion. 30 shades on PDP is suspected to cause decision paralysis. Notable because Foundation Stick is a launch the campaign is leaning into.

    Reach is being purchased, conversion isn't following. Matches the macro CVR collapse pattern. Worth A/B testing a "Pick your shade" funnel or quiz-first PDP.
  • Audience Network delivery issues

    Sam Brady flagged in Slack on May 5 and May 18. Some advertisers reporting issues. Not confirmed widespread.

    Audience Network breakdown is available in Meta — quick check: does the spend share on AN spike in March-May?
  • Creative mix: video crushing static, Jonesy underperforming

    Standup: video (UGC + partnership) dramatically outperforming static. Jonesy content spent $50K in 7 days without beating cheaper internal content. Static-heavy weeks may correlate with the steeper CVR drops.

    Worth pulling spend share by creative_type weekly and overlaying on this chart.
  • Partnership Ads diminishing returns

    Tommy on April 23: Partnerships (VO) 0.40 ncROAS vs Cost Cap 0.55 ncROAS — recommended reallocation toward Cost Cap. Partnerships still drove 60-70% of Meta spend at the time.

    If Partnerships ROAS slipped from 1.0+ to 0.4 while Partnerships was the dominant spend bucket, that alone moves the blended number meaningfully.
  • Website / landing-page conversion

    Standup flagged site speed and landing page experience as a "divide and conquer" audit item. Cody — worth pulling Heatmap CVR by page for the same window.

    Easy ask: /heatmap on top JRB LPs to see if site-side CVR moved.
