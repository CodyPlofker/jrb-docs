Interim Findings

n = 32 completed responses · Field period: 2026-05-13 → 2026-05-14 · Source: Typeform ETOHbbPw

Quiz is best-in-class on questions and model photos (avg confidence 7.8/10, 77% promoters) — but the single most fixable issue is that ~one-third of respondents only see ONE product recommendation on the results page. The additional matches are either missing, hidden below the fold, or not visually surfaced. Undertone remains the #1 source of mid-quiz confusion.

Top Issues (Ranked)

  1. Critical Users only see ONE product recommendation — additional matches are missing, hidden below the fold, or not visually surfaced
    Mentioned across 10 responses · Mobile referrals from Facebook are most affected · "I only received one recommendation"
  2. Critical Undertone question (cool/neutral/warm) is the #1 confusion point — the burn/tan logic feels wrong and terms are undefined
    8 mentions · "a warm undertone says I tan easily. Absolutely not!" · multiple users took quiz twice to test both options
  3. Critical Shade match returns wrong color (too dark, too warm, or contradicts in-store / past selfie quiz results)
    5 mentions · Includes inconsistency vs. in-store match and past quizzes (Medium vs Medium Honey)
  4. High Color-family question (pale vs fair vs light) stalls users at the start of the quiz
    5 mentions · "I am always stuck at one of the first questions, i.e. my color family. Am I pale or fair?"
  5. High Model photo set doesn't represent every user — missing redheads, silver/grey hair, older skin, certain undertones
    5 mentions · "very few redheads that are not super fair" · "models closer to my age range with silver grey hair"
  6. High Sample-pack / try-before-buy is the #1 missing purchase driver for non-loyalists
    5 mentions, all from price-sensitive shoppers · "$5–$10 sample card to defray shipping" is a recurring ask
  7. High Results-page rationale blurb has a copy-bug — describes wrong skin type/undertone for the user
    1 mention but visible bug: "We found your shade in these products too. They work well with combination skin that's deep with neutral undertones..." returned to a user who is not deep/neutral.
  8. Medium Results page lacks "why this product vs that" rationale (coverage level, when to use which)
    3 mentions · Want copy like "For more full coverage, use X. For touch-ups, use X."
  9. Medium Quiz scope is unclear at start — users assume it's Foundation-Stick-only
    2 mentions · "I just assumed it was only for the new foundation stick"
  10. Low Reviews + press-logo interstitial screens between quiz and results feel inefficient
    "The extra screens are unnecessary and doesn't build anticipation or affect my buying decisions"

Recommended Next Steps

  • P1 · Fix
    Debug the multi-product results display. Reproduce the "only WTF appeared" state — most reports were mobile referrals from Facebook in-app browser. Verify additional product cards are rendering and not hidden below a primary-result hero, then re-balance the visual hierarchy (main rec smaller, alternatives equally prominent).
  • P1 · Fix
    Patch the results-page rationale copy-bug. The "Other Matches to Round Out Your Routine" sentence appears to be mis-populating skin-type / undertone tokens against the wrong customer.
  • P1 · Re-write
    Rewrite the undertone question + helper text. Decouple "burns easily / tans easily" from undertone (warm undertones can absolutely burn). Add a one-line definition of cool/neutral/warm with a visual cue (vein color, jewelry, sun reaction) rather than a binary photo.
  • P2
    Add "why this product?" rationale on the results page. A 1-sentence blurb under each recommended product explaining coverage level, finish, or use-case.
  • P2
    Add a sample-pack option ($5–$10) to the results CTA — 5 explicit requests, all from non-loyalist segments. Strongest fence-sitter conversion lever.
  • P2
    Diversify model photo set. Add redheads, silver/grey-hair models, older-skin examples, and the missing Medium Honey shade option in the medium/medium-dark family.
  • P3
    Reframe the quiz intro. Welcome screen / first slide should make it clear this quiz now covers WTF, JETM, Foundation Stick, and powder — not just Foundation Stick.
  • P3
    Cross-link to MB and Blush quizzes from the results page — repeated suggestion, easy bundle/upsell.
  • P3
    Cut or shorten the reviews + press-logo interstitial screens between quiz and results.
